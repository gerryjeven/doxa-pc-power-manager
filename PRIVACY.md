# Privacy Policy: Doxa PC Power Manager

*Last updated: October 29, 2026*

Doxa PC Power Manager ("the app") is a utility that reads and changes processor power settings on your computer.
This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Information we collect
**None.** The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising and no crash reporting.

## What stays on your computer
- The app reads your processor, power plan and battery status to show them on screen and to apply the settings you
  choose. This information never leaves your computer.
- Your preferences (language and light/dark theme) are saved locally on your computer only.
- The app's window talks to a small service that runs on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`). It is not reachable from
  the internet or from other devices.

## Internet connections
- **Microsoft Store version:** the app does not connect to the internet. Updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store.
- **Version downloaded from GitHub:** to check for updates, the app requests the latest release page from
  `github.com`. Like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and the app's version number. No other data is
  sent. GitHub's handling of this data is covered by the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: open an issue at
https://github.com/gerryjeven/doxa-pc-power-manager/issues

# Kebijakan Privasi: Doxa PC Power Manager

*Terakhir diperbarui: 29 Oktober 2026*

Doxa PC Power Manager ("aplikasi") adalah utilitas untuk membaca dan mengubah setelan daya prosesor di komputer Anda.

## Data yang dikumpulkan
**Tidak ada.** Aplikasi tidak mengumpulkan, menyimpan, menjual, atau membagikan data pribadi apa pun. Tidak ada akun,
analitik, iklan, maupun laporan crash.

## Yang tetap di komputer Anda
- Informasi prosesor, power plan, dan baterai hanya dibaca untuk ditampilkan dan untuk menerapkan setelan yang Anda
  pilih. Informasi ini tidak pernah keluar dari komputer Anda.
- Preferensi (bahasa dan tema) hanya disimpan di komputer Anda.
- Jendela aplikasi berkomunikasi dengan layanan kecil di komputer Anda sendiri (`127.0.0.1`) yang tidak bisa diakses
  dari internet maupun perangkat lain.

## Koneksi internet
- **Versi Microsoft Store:** aplikasi tidak terhubung ke internet. Update dikirim oleh Microsoft Store.
- **Versi unduhan GitHub:** untuk memeriksa update, aplikasi membuka halaman rilis terbaru di `github.com`. Seperti
  permintaan web pada umumnya, GitHub menerima alamat IP Anda dan nomor versi aplikasi. Tidak ada data lain yang dikirim.

## Kontak
Pertanyaan tentang kebijakan ini: buka issue di https://github.com/gerryjeven/doxa-pc-power-manager/issues
